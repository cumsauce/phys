Click to strike at this speed, or flick: press, sweep and release, and the sweep's speed sets how hard.
Click a point to pluck it, or drag across strings to strum.
Hold the mouse down on a part to bow it; slide to move the bow, scroll to press harder, let go to lift. Near one end of a string works best.

Keys

A play point is a spot bound to a key or a MIDI note. Right-click a spot to add one. Shift plays harder, Alt softer. Right-drag turns the view while this tool is on.
